CRAFTSMANSHIP & SPECIALIZATION

Services Built Around Precision Results

We focus on what we do best: framing, hanging drywall, taping, finishing corners, and restoring damaged interior surfaces to flawless condition.

01

Drywall Installation, Taping & Finishing

Whether you are putting up drywall in a new garage, finishing a multi-room addition, or drywalling a newly built custom home, the quality of the finish depends on precision taping and mudding.

We ensure perfectly flat butt joints, crisp corner beads, and smooth, invisible seams that make painting effortless. We treat every wall as if it was in our own house.

  • Sheetrock hanging for walls and ceilings
  • Multi-coat mud application and hand/pole sanding
  • Level 4 and Level 5 smooth wall finishes
  • Fire-rated drywall installation (Firetape compliance)

"His work is excellent and his corners and butts are beautiful! He sings when he works so you know he loves what he does!"

John Jablonka, Garage Finishing Client
02

Drywall & Ceiling Water Damage Repairs

Ice dams, roof leaks, and plumbing mishaps can destroy drywall ceilings and leave dangerous holes or crumbling plaster.

We fix water damage seamlessly, replacing ruined insulation and drywall, patching cracks, and matching existing textures. We also install custom plumbing access panels so you are prepared if pipe work is ever required again.

  • Ceiling crack and sag repairs
  • Ice dam damage and water leak drywall replacement
  • Plaster repair and drywall integration
  • Custom access panel design and installation

"Ed not only seamlessly repaired the damage; but on our request designed and installed an access panel to the upstairs pipes..."

Karen F. Johnson, Syracuse NY
03

Wood & Metal Stud Framing

A straight, plumb wall starts with solid structural framing. We build partition walls, closet enclosures, door openings, and room dividers that are perfectly square.

Whether framing with traditional lumber or lightweight structural metal studs for commercial projects, our framing sets the foundation for clean drywall execution.

  • Interior partition wall construction
  • Soffit removal and ceiling framing reconfigurations
  • Doorway rough opening framing and header construction
  • Commercial metal stud track installation
04

Basement & Attic Transformations

Unlock the full potential of your home by converting unfinished basements or raw attics into bedrooms, entertainment rooms, or home offices.

We handle the entire interior build out: framing perimeter walls around utility lines, fitting around tight ductwork, insulating, and installing drywall on vaulted ceiling angles.

  • Full basement bedroom and living room build-outs
  • Attic sloped ceiling and dormer drywalling
  • Kitchen and bathroom remodeling drywall prep
  • Insulation installation and vapor barrier sealing
